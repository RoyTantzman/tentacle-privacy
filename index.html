Tentacle

Tentacle is a personal email app for Windows, used only by its author, Roy Tantzman.

Privacy policy

Tentacle reads, organizes and sends the author's own Gmail through Google's Gmail API, entirely on his own computer. Mail and sign-in tokens are stored only on that computer. No data is collected, sent to any other server, sold or shared with anyone.

Tentacle's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
